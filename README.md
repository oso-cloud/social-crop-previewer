# Social Crop Previewer

Preview exactly how your photos and videos will be cropped on TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook — before you post.

**Use it here: https://oso-cloud.github.io/social-crop-previewer/**

- Import a photo or video (stays on your device — nothing is uploaded)
- Pick a platform & format: TikTok posts/stories, IG reels/stories/posts/carousels, FB posts/covers
- See red overlays where the app UI (username, icons, caption/nav) will cover your content
- Drag / pinch / scroll to reframe, with a rule-of-thirds grid and resolution quality check
- Export a correctly sized PNG of the exact crop
